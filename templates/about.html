Eugene Baldomar

Managing Director

Eugene Baldomar is the Managing Director of {{ company.name }}, leading the company's mission to deliver trusted calibration and metrology solutions across Singapore and the region. A graduate of the University of Saint La Salle with a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical, Electronics & Communications Engineering, Eugene brings strong expertise in calibration, pharmaceutical technology, and process validation.

He has completed advanced professional training in:

  • Environmental Chamber Calibration

  • Pharma Technology Equipment Validation Workshops
  • Transformational Leadership Development

By combining technical mastery with strategic leadership, Eugene ensures that KSA Metrology continues to uphold the highest standards of quality, innovation, and customer trust.

Roedil Gelera

Director

Roedil Gelera is the Director of {{ company.name }}, overseeing the company's strategic direction and technical excellence in calibration and metrology services. A graduate of the University of Saint La Salle with a Bachelor's Degree in Electronics and Communications Engineering, Roedil brings nearly two decades of experience in ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory competence, electrical and mechanical calibration, and advanced measurement systems.

He has completed specialized training and certifications with international institutions including ASTAR, IAS, Fluke Corporation, Rice Lake Weighing Systems, and Singapore Test Services, covering:

  • Industrial Temperature Measurement – Resistance Thermometry
  • Vacuum & Differential Pressure Calibration
  • Electrical & Mechanical Metrology
  • Laboratory Management
  • Kaizen Member Training

Roedil's leadership blends technical mastery with a commitment to quality and innovation, ensuring that KSA Metrology remains a trusted partner for clients across Singapore and the region.
